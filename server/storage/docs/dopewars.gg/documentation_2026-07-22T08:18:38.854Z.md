DOPE
====

Governance token and base primitive of the Dope Wars metaverse,

8,000 randomized, limited-edition NFT bundles of $PAPER and Gear were released September 2021 during a gas-only mint.  
  
Each DOPE provides an equal Governance Vote on Proposals from the DAO.

[#### Get DOPE](https://app.dopewars.gg/swap-meet)

Hustlers
========

Playable characters for Dope Wars

Hustlers are bleeding edge, fully-customizable in-game characters made by Claiming Gear from an original DOPE then minting a Hustler on the Optimism network.  
  
All 2D Hustler pixel artwork is stored on the blockchain and can be changed at any time using our Swap Meet.

[#### Get a Hustler](https://app.dopewars.gg/swap-meet/hustlers)

Gear
====

Swappable equipment for Hustlers

Interchangeable pieces of equipment that live on the L2 Optimism blockchain as ERC-1155 tokens. They are created by Claiming an original DOPE NFT.  
  
This claim process produces 9 separate NFT items that can be traded and equipped independently of one another on Optimism.

[#### Get some Gear](https://app.dopewars.gg/swap-meet/gear)

$PAPER
======

In-game virtual currency

PAPER is the ecosystem currency of Dope Wars. It was originally distributed through a one-time claimable amount of 125,000 per DOPE.

It lives on the Ethereum and Optimism networks.  
  
PAPER is necessary to “Claim Gear” from a DOPE, and will be used in future experiences.
