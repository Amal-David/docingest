Frequently Asked Questions
--------------------------

[Are the ÖSD Exam recognized for Visa Applications??](https://grmanexams.lk/#collapse-d1c0bb86a8c1adc7b159)

Yes, the exam is recognized by the German, Austrian and Swiss consulates and Embassies all over the world. It is proof for applying for all types of Visa and Resident Permits in these countries.

[Are the ÖSD Exams applicable when applying for Higher Education in Germany?](https://grmanexams.lk/#collapse-f0bbca96a8c1adc7b159)

Yes, the exams are recognized by public and private universities in Germany. ÖSD is recognized by universities in over 40 countries.  www.osd.at/en/exams/worldwide-recognition. For further information regarding higher education in Germany, Sri Lanka’s number one company for Higher Education in Germany, www.studyingermany.lk

[How can I prepare for the ÖSD Exams?](https://grmanexams.lk/#collapse-3628f066a8c1adc7b159)

If you have the knowledge of the level, then you can face the ÖSD Exam. There is no strict Textbooks you should follow. We provide free practice material and our Language Institute Grman.lk also have an Exam preparation class. Students who studied in the Grman.lk 

[How can i make the payment?](https://grmanexams.lk/#collapse-f0320f86a8c1adc7b159)

We offer three payment options. Direct Bank Transfers, Online Card Payments or face to face cash payments. (Only on certain days)

[Until when Can I register?](https://grmanexams.lk/#collapse-9881b426a8c1adc7b159)

The Exact registration days will depend on the timelines, but in general, an exam taker can register up to 30 days before the exam.

[What will be my proof of registration?](https://grmanexams.lk/#collapse-ad916c26a8c1adc7bb67)

You will be sent an email confirmation after you register for the exams. This will be presented digitally or printed during the examinations.

[How long will it take to receive my results?](https://grmanexams.lk/#collapse-26015116a8c1adc7bb67)

You will receive your results after a minimum of 4 working weeks. 

[What will be my proof of passing my Exam?](https://grmanexams.lk/#collapse-99823eb6a8c1adc7bb67)

ÖSD will issue a printed and digital certification for passing your German Exams. For more information with regard to the recognition of your certifications. www.osd.at/en/exams/worldwide-recognition/

[Will I have to retake the whole exam if i fail a level?](https://grmanexams.lk/#collapse-8209d426a8c1adc7bb67)

No, the ÖSD Examinations starting from the A1 Zertifikat model are broken down to speaking and writing examinations. You will only have to repeat this particular part of the exam. 

[What is the comparison of ÖSD ZERTIFIKAT to GOETHE ZERTIFIKAT?](https://grmanexams.lk/#collapse-56e57126a8c1adc7bb67)

ÖSD ZERTIFIKAT is a direct comparison for GOETHE ZERTIFIKAT. For example, ÖSD Zertifikat A1 t Goethe-Zertifikat A1: Start Deutsch 1. For Further Information please refer to www.osd.at/en/exams/oesd-exams-by-international-standards/

# ÖSD German A1 & A2 Exam Passing Criteria

ÖSD German Exam Passing Criteria — A1 and A2 Complete Guide
===========================================================

*   [June 22, 2026](https://grmanexams.lk/2026/06/22/)
    

*   [Blog](https://grmanexams.lk/category/blog/)
    

![](https://grmanexams.lk/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/360_F_554631911_nlgHPOZcvDk0rdYOdVQxgg1mC0MuIAfg.jpg)

What is the ÖSD German Exam?
----------------------------

[The ÖSD (Österreichisches Sprachdiplom Deutsch)](https://www.osd.at/en/)
 is an internationally recognized German language certification. Grman Exams is Sri Lanka’s only official ÖSD examination centre, with branches in Colombo, Kandy, and Jaffna. The A1 and A2 levels are the entry-level certifications, commonly required for [Au Pair visa](https://studyingermany.lk/au-pair-germany/)
 and [Ausbildung applications](https://studyingermany.lk/ausbildung-visa/)
 in Germany.

ÖSD A1 and A2 Exam Structure
----------------------------

Both the A1 and A2 exams consist of two main parts: 

*   **Written exam** — covers Lesen (Reading), Hören (Listening), and Schreiben (Writing)
*   **Speaking exam (Sprechen)** — conducted separately

Students can sit both parts together or take them separately. If a student fails one part, they only need to retake that specific part — not the whole exam.

ÖSD A1 Exam — Passing Criteria
------------------------------

To pass the ÖSD A1 written exam, students must score a minimum of **38 out of 75 marks**. However, simply reaching the total is not enough — each component also has a mandatory minimum mark.

### A1 Written Exam — Component Breakdown

*   **Lesen (Reading)** – Maximum 30 marks can be achieved with a mandatory minimum mark of 6 marks.
*   **Hören (Listening)** – Maximum 30 marks can be achieved with a mandatory minimum mark of 6 marks.
*   **Schreiben 1 (Writing)** – Maximum 5 marks can be achieved.
*   **Schreiben 2 (Writing)** – Maximum 10 marks can be achieved with a mandatory minimum mark of 4 marks.

### A1 Speaking Exam (Sprechen)

The A1 speaking exam carries a maximum of **25 marks**. Students must achieve a minimum of **12 marks** to pass this component.

ÖSD A2 Exam — Passing Criteria
------------------------------

To pass the ÖSD A2 written exam, students must score a minimum of **35 out of 70 marks**, along with meeting the minimum mark in each component.

### A2 Written Exam — Component Breakdown

*   **Lesen (Reading)** – Maximum 25 marks can be achieved with a mandatory minimum mark of 5 marks.
*   **Hören (Listening)** – Maximum 30 marks can be achieved with a mandatory minimum mark of 6 marks.
*   **Schreiben (Writing)** – Maximum 15 marks can be achieved with a mandatory minimum mark of 3 marks.

### A2 Speaking Exam (Sprechen)

The A2 speaking exam carries a maximum of **20 marks**. Students must achieve a minimum of **10 marks** to pass this component.

What is the ÖSD German Certificate?

The ÖSD — Österreichisches Sprachdiplom Deutsch — is an internationally recognised German language examination system based on the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages (CEFR). Frequently Asked Questions

[Are the ÖSD Exam recognized for Visa Applications??](https://grmanexams.lk/2026/06/24/#collapse-d1c0bb86a8c1adc7b159)

Yes, the exam is recognized by the German, Austrian and Swiss consulates and Embassies all over the world. It is proof for applying for all types of Visa and Resident Permits in these countries.

[Are the ÖSD Exams applicable when applying for Higher Education in Germany?](https://grmanexams.lk/2026/06/24/#collapse-f0bbca96a8c1adc7b159)

Yes, the exams are recognized by public and private universities in Germany. ÖSD is recognized by universities in over 40 countries.  www.osd.at/en/exams/worldwide-recognition. For further information regarding higher education in Germany, Sri Lanka’s number one company for Higher Education in Germany, www.studyingermany.lk

[How can I prepare for the ÖSD Exams?](https://grmanexams.lk/2026/06/24/#collapse-3628f066a8c1adc7b159)

If you have the knowledge of the level, then you can face the ÖSD Exam. There is no strict Textbooks you should follow. We provide free practice material and our Language Institute Grman.lk also have an Exam preparation class. Students who studied in the Grman.lk 

[How can i make the payment?](https://grmanexams.lk/2026/06/24/#collapse-f0320f86a8c1adc7b159)

We offer three payment options. Direct Bank Transfers, Online Card Payments or face to face cash payments. (Only on certain days)

[Until when Can I register?](https://grmanexams.lk/2026/06/24/#collapse-9881b426a8c1adc7b159)

The Exact registration days will depend on the timelines, but in general, an exam taker can register up to 30 days before the exam.

[What will be my proof of registration?](https://grmanexams.lk/2026/06/24/#collapse-ad916c26a8c1adc7bb67)

You will be sent an email confirmation after you register for the exams. This will be presented digitally or printed during the examinations.

[How long will it take to receive my results?](https://grmanexams.lk/2026/06/24/#collapse-26015116a8c1adc7bb67)

You will receive your results after a minimum of 4 working weeks. 

[What will be my proof of passing my Exam?](https://grmanexams.lk/2026/06/24/#collapse-99823eb6a8c1adc7bb67)

ÖSD will issue a printed and digital certification for passing your German Exams. For more information with regard to the recognition of your certifications. www.osd.at/en/exams/worldwide-recognition/

[Will I have to retake the whole exam if i fail a level?](https://grmanexams.lk/2026/06/24/#collapse-8209d426a8c1adc7bb67)

No, the ÖSD Examinations starting from the A1 Zertifikat model are broken down to speaking and writing examinations. You will only have to repeat this particular part of the exam. 

[What is the comparison of ÖSD ZERTIFIKAT to GOETHE ZERTIFIKAT?](https://grmanexams.lk/2026/06/24/#collapse-56e57126a8c1adc7bb67)

ÖSD ZERTIFIKAT is a direct comparison for GOETHE ZERTIFIKAT. For example, ÖSD Zertifikat A1 t Goethe-Zertifikat A1: Start Deutsch 1. For Further Information please refer to www.osd.at/en/exams/oesd-exams-by-international-standards/

Frequently Asked Questions
--------------------------

[Are the ÖSD Exam recognized for Visa Applications??](https://grmanexams.lk/2026/07/01/#collapse-d1c0bb86a8c1adc7b159)

Yes, the exam is recognized by the German, Austrian and Swiss consulates and Embassies all over the world. It is proof for applying for all types of Visa and Resident Permits in these countries.

[Are the ÖSD Exams applicable when applying for Higher Education in Germany?](https://grmanexams.lk/2026/07/01/#collapse-f0bbca96a8c1adc7b159)

Yes, the exams are recognized by public and private universities in Germany. ÖSD is recognized by universities in over 40 countries.  www.osd.at/en/exams/worldwide-recognition. For further information regarding higher education in Germany, Sri Lanka’s number one company for Higher Education in Germany, www.studyingermany.lk

[How can I prepare for the ÖSD Exams?](https://grmanexams.lk/2026/07/01/#collapse-3628f066a8c1adc7b159)

If you have the knowledge of the level, then you can face the ÖSD Exam. There is no strict Textbooks you should follow. We provide free practice material and our Language Institute Grman.lk also have an Exam preparation class. Students who studied in the Grman.lk 

[How can i make the payment?](https://grmanexams.lk/2026/07/01/#collapse-f0320f86a8c1adc7b159)

We offer three payment options. Direct Bank Transfers, Online Card Payments or face to face cash payments. (Only on certain days)

[Until when Can I register?](https://grmanexams.lk/2026/07/01/#collapse-9881b426a8c1adc7b159)

The Exact registration days will depend on the timelines, but in general, an exam taker can register up to 30 days before the exam.

[What will be my proof of registration?](https://grmanexams.lk/2026/07/01/#collapse-ad916c26a8c1adc7bb67)

You will be sent an email confirmation after you register for the exams. This will be presented digitally or printed during the examinations.

[How long will it take to receive my results?](https://grmanexams.lk/2026/07/01/#collapse-26015116a8c1adc7bb67)

You will receive your results after a minimum of 4 working weeks. 

[What will be my proof of passing my Exam?](https://grmanexams.lk/2026/07/01/#collapse-99823eb6a8c1adc7bb67)

ÖSD will issue a printed and digital certification for passing your German Exams. For more information with regard to the recognition of your certifications. www.osd.at/en/exams/worldwide-recognition/

[Will I have to retake the whole exam if i fail a level?](https://grmanexams.lk/2026/07/01/#collapse-8209d426a8c1adc7bb67)

No, the ÖSD Examinations starting from the A1 Zertifikat model are broken down to speaking and writing examinations. You will only have to repeat this particular part of the exam. 

[What is the comparison of ÖSD ZERTIFIKAT to GOETHE ZERTIFIKAT?](https://grmanexams.lk/2026/07/01/#collapse-56e57126a8c1adc7bb67)

ÖSD ZERTIFIKAT is a direct comparison for GOETHE ZERTIFIKAT. For example, ÖSD Zertifikat A1 t Goethe-Zertifikat A1: Start Deutsch 1. For Further Information please refer to www.osd.at/en/exams/oesd-exams-by-international-standards/

Frequently Asked Questions
--------------------------

[Are the ÖSD Exam recognized for Visa Applications??](https://grmanexams.lk/2026/07/03/#collapse-d1c0bb86a8c1adc7b159)

Yes, the exam is recognized by the German, Austrian and Swiss consulates and Embassies all over the world. It is proof for applying for all types of Visa and Resident Permits in these countries.

[Are the ÖSD Exams applicable when applying for Higher Education in Germany?](https://grmanexams.lk/2026/07/03/#collapse-f0bbca96a8c1adc7b159)

Yes, the exams are recognized by public and private universities in Germany. ÖSD is recognized by universities in over 40 countries.  www.osd.at/en/exams/worldwide-recognition. For further information regarding higher education in Germany, Sri Lanka’s number one company for Higher Education in Germany, www.studyingermany.lk

[How can I prepare for the ÖSD Exams?](https://grmanexams.lk/2026/07/03/#collapse-3628f066a8c1adc7b159)

If you have the knowledge of the level, then you can face the ÖSD Exam. There is no strict Textbooks you should follow. We provide free practice material and our Language Institute Grman.lk also have an Exam preparation class. Students who studied in the Grman.lk 

[How can i make the payment?](https://grmanexams.lk/2026/07/03/#collapse-f0320f86a8c1adc7b159)

We offer three payment options. Direct Bank Transfers, Online Card Payments or face to face cash payments. (Only on certain days)

[Until when Can I register?](https://grmanexams.lk/2026/07/03/#collapse-9881b426a8c1adc7b159)

The Exact registration days will depend on the timelines, but in general, an exam taker can register up to 30 days before the exam.

[What will be my proof of registration?](https://grmanexams.lk/2026/07/03/#collapse-ad916c26a8c1adc7bb67)

You will be sent an email confirmation after you register for the exams. This will be presented digitally or printed during the examinations.

[How long will it take to receive my results?](https://grmanexams.lk/2026/07/03/#collapse-26015116a8c1adc7bb67)

You will receive your results after a minimum of 4 working weeks. 

[What will be my proof of passing my Exam?](https://grmanexams.lk/2026/07/03/#collapse-99823eb6a8c1adc7bb67)

ÖSD will issue a printed and digital certification for passing your German Exams. For more information with regard to the recognition of your certifications. www.osd.at/en/exams/worldwide-recognition/

[Will I have to retake the whole exam if i fail a level?](https://grmanexams.lk/2026/07/03/#collapse-8209d426a8c1adc7bb67)

No, the ÖSD Examinations starting from the A1 Zertifikat model are broken down to speaking and writing examinations. You will only have to repeat this particular part of the exam. 

[What is the comparison of ÖSD ZERTIFIKAT to GOETHE ZERTIFIKAT?](https://grmanexams.lk/2026/07/03/#collapse-56e57126a8c1adc7bb67)

ÖSD ZERTIFIKAT is a direct comparison for GOETHE ZERTIFIKAT. For example, ÖSD Zertifikat A1 t Goethe-Zertifikat A1: Start Deutsch 1. For Further Information please refer to www.osd.at/en/exams/oesd-exams-by-international-standards/
