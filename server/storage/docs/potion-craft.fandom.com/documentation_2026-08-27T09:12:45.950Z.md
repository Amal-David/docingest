Learn the Art of Alchemy

_**[Potion Craft](https://potion-craft.fandom.com/wiki/Potion_Craft "Potion Craft")
**_ is an alchemist simulator developed by **niceplay games** for PC, with a planned release date of 2021. Play as an aspiring alchemist in your own potion shop, where you phsycially interact with your tools and ingredients to brew potions. Start your journey down [The Alchemist's Path](https://potion-craft.fandom.com/wiki/The_Alchemist%27s_Path "The Alchemist's Path")
, invent new [recipes](https://potion-craft.fandom.com/wiki/Crafting "Crafting")
, attract [customers](https://potion-craft.fandom.com/wiki/Customers "Customers")
, [experiement](https://potion-craft.fandom.com/wiki/Effects "Effects")
 to your hearts content, and forge your own destiny to become the alchemist this town needs!
